## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an ethereal, sculptural, and avant-garde computing paradigm. Inspired by natural artificial intelligence and biomimetic calm computing, it reimagines the desktop environment not as a series of rigid operational windows, but as a living canvas of soft, ambient forms suspended in space.

### Core Tenets
- **Sculptural Fluidity:** Interface surfaces behave like micro-molded physical materials coated in ambient mist, reacting subtly to illumination and interaction.
- **Sparse High-Signal Clarity:** Negative space is treated as an active functional component. Interfaces present only the essential signals, aggressively suppressing noise to promote unhurried cognitive focus.
- **Luminous Equilibrium:** Instead of harsh drop-shadows or stark neon contrasts, depth is produced through diffuse atmospheric color fields, specular light reflections, and frosted soft-surface glass.
- **Dynamic Bento Asymmetry:** Workspaces abandon symmetrical modular grids in favor of organic, rhythmic masonry and focal bento arrangements that elevate content to museum-grade composition.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ethereal, mist-diffused daylight canvas punctuated by pinpoint electric energies. 

- **Canvas & Surface System:** 
  - Canvas Root: `#F4F6FB` (Soft Atmospheric Mist)
  - Card & Container Base: `#EBF0F8` (Sculpted Mid-Mist)
  - Elevated Plate / Specular Face: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Crystalline White)
- **Signal Accents:**
  - Primary Electric Indigo (`#4F46E5`): Reserved for active states, high-priority intents, and authoritative system markers.
  - Secondary Radiant Cyan (`#06B6D4`): Expresses live telemetry, ambient data threads, real-time sync, and sensory AI focus.
  - Tertiary Ethereal Violet (`#818CF8`): Soft transitional accents and multi-state glows.
- **Neutrals & Legibility:**
  - Ink Solid (`#0F172A`): High-signal typography and dense structural data.
  - Ink Subdued (`#64748B`): Secondary descriptors, inactive units, and navigational breadcrumbs.
  - Specular Rim (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`): 1px inner edge illumination creating physical mass.
  - Shadow Tint (`rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.04)` to `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`): Low-density, ultra-diffused shadow blooms.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is engineered to support an ultra-refined, anti-clutter aesthetic. 

- **Display & Headlines (Sora):** Used sparingly to establish monumental focal points. Light weights (`300` to `400`) at scale lend an airy architectural quality, while tight negative letter spacing maintains cohesiveness across spatial clusters.
- **Body & Prose (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Chosen for its humanistic openness, geometric structure, and organic terminal curves that mirror the rounded contours of the interface. High line-height ratios preserve whitespace within dense information panes.
- **Technical Readouts & System Chips (JetBrains Mono):** Injected strictly for precision metrics, file sizes, spatial coordinates, and algorithmic readouts. Set with wide letter tracking (`0.08em`) to balance the organic nature of the surrounding typography.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts reject rigid, monotonous grids in favor of an asymmetrical, fluid Bento framework structured around focal prominence and natural eye sweeps.

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views operate on an adaptive 12-column variable Bento grid where primary focal modules occupy larger footprints (e.g., 7 or 8 columns) surrounded by rhythmic vertical spans (e.g., 4 or 5 columns).
- **Breathing Canvas:** Padding inside primary panels is intentionally hyper-generous—consistently `2rem` to `3rem` (`space-xl` to `space-3xl`)—preventing UI elements from crowding the edges and allowing the background atmospheric gradients to breathe.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile (`< 768px`): 4-column flow with reduced outer margin (`1.5rem`), single-stack cards, and docked pill navigation.
  - Tablet (`768px - 1199px`): 8-column layout with standard `1.5rem` gutters.
  - Desktop (`≥ 1200px`): Full 12-column dynamic Bento layout with `2rem` gutters and minimum `3rem` margins.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use mechanical multi-tier drop shadows. Depth is achieved through light refraction, layered translucency, and surface luminescence.

- **The Mist Underlay:** The root desktop canvas is textured with ultra-soft ambient blurs—giant, slow-moving radial patches of light indigo and cyan (`filter: blur(120px)`) shifting subtly beneath the workspace.
- **Frosted Soft-Surface Plates:** Interactive modules utilize semi-opaque fills (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` to `rgba(235, 240, 248, 0.85)`) over `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(160%)`.
- **Specular Precision Rim:** To prevent surfaces from melting into the background, every card features an internal specular boundary: `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` coupled with a perimeter hair-rule of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)`.
- **Luminous Atmospheric Shadows:** When elements float or hover, shadows extend into diffused fields:
  - Default: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.03)`
  - Elevated/Active: `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.08), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.06)`

## Shapes

The geometric signature is defined by continuous curvature, pill contours, and tactile soft-molded perimeters that feel pebble-smooth and natural.

- **Component Envelopes:** Cards and primary system tiles employ `rounded-3xl` (24px to 32px), creating fluid silhouettes that contrast gently against screen edges.
- **Controls & Active Targets:** Buttons, tags, chips, and command inputs adhere strictly to full pill profiles (`rounded-full`), accentuating the kinetic feeling of handheld instruments.
- **Nested Curvature Harmonization:** When controls are placed inside containers, their radii step down proportionally (e.g., 32px card radius contains 16px child surfaces and pill-shaped action triggers) to preserve natural visual rhythm.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action:** Solid pill element featuring `#4F46E5` background, high-contrast white text, subtle specular upper border (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`), and a hovering ambient cyan-indigo aura.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Frosted translucent pill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`) with `1px border-white/80`, text in Ink Solid (`#0F172A`).
- **Interactive Feedback:** Micro-spring physical compression (`scale: 0.98`) upon click with smooth deceleration (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).

### Bento Cards & Floating Surfaces
- **Structure:** Encased in sculpted mist white plates with padding from `32px` to `48px`.
- **Header Structure:** Sparse hierarchy. A single `label-caps` indicator paired with an expressive `headline-md` title, maximizing unencumbered whitespace.
- **Inner Borders:** Crisp 1px perimeter using `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` on top and left borders, tapering into `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)` on bottom and right to simulate top-down ambient light.

### Chips & Sensor Badges
- **Form:** Ultra-compact pill contours enclosing `label-caps` typography.
- **Styling:** Faint cyan or indigo tinted backgrounds (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.08)` or `rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.08)`) with a 6px breathing dot indicator pulsing softly for active status.

### Form Inputs & Query Bars
- **Style:** Deeply rounded pill containers with a recessed matte surface (`rgba(235, 240, 248, 0.6)`) and inset light shadowing.
- **Focus State:** Transitions effortlessly into pure white (`#FFFFFF`) backed by an inner specular highlight and a gentle halo glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.15)`). No aggressive outlines.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- **Structure:** Borderless, zero-divider execution. Rows are separated entirely through generous vertical rhythm (`space-md` gaps) and soft contextual hover halos (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)`).

### Toggle Switches & Selection Controls
- **Toggles:** Soft organic track capsules with an illuminated tactile marble thumb that slides smoothly with momentum. Active tracks switch to an electric indigo/cyan linear gradient.
- **Checkboxes/Radios:** Pill-derived squircle shapes featuring subtle inner specular glows and clean cyan tick marks.
